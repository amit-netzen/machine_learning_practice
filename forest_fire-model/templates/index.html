Process

Three steps from field reading to forecast

01

Enter conditions

Temperature, relative humidity, wind speed, rainfall, and the FFMC, DMC and ISI fuel codes for the day.

02

Model scales & predicts

Inputs are standardized with the fitted scaler, then passed through the trained ridge regression model.

03

Read the index

The predicted Fire Weather Index is returned instantly — higher values mean greater fire danger.
